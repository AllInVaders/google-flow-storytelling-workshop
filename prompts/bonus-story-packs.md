# Bonus Google Flow Story Packs (Omni Flash + `@Character` Workflow)

> **Purpose**: Ready-to-run alternate commercial story packs for **Google Flow** ([labs.google/fx/tools/flow](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow)) using the exact same **5-Step Incremental Workflow**:
> 1. **Step 1**: Short Story Seed & 5-Shot Beat Sheet (`Google Flow Agent`)
> 2. **Step 2**: Create Characters Once (`Characters > New Character` with bundled Voice) & Scene Ingredients (`Image > Nano Banana Pro`)
> 3. **Step 3**: Storyboard Frames using `@Character` & `@Ingredient` tags (`Nano Banana Pro`)
> 4. **Step 4**: Video & Dialogue Animation (`Video > Omni Flash`)
> 5. **Step 5**: Conversational Video Edit (`Omni Flash` 3-turn editor) & **Scenebuilder**

---

## Bonus Pack A: "NovaPay — The Street-Market Violinist" (Tap-to-Tip App)

### Step 1: 2-Sentence Story Seed

```text
At dusk in a bustling cobblestone plaza, street violinist Elena finishes a passionate performance only to see a commuter realize he has no cash in his wallet. With a quick smile, he taps his phone against her NovaPay stand, triggering an instant chime that lights up her evening.
```

### Step 2: Create Characters & Ingredients Once in Google Flow

#### Characters (`Left Sidebar > Characters > New Character`)

- **`@Elena`** (Voice: *Expressive Young Female Soprano*)
  ```text
  26-year-old street violinist with warm bronze skin, dark braided hair wrapped in a crown, expressive dark-brown eyes, wearing an emerald-green wool coat and a cream knit scarf, neutral 5600K studio lighting, clean slate-gray background, photorealistic portrait.
  ```
- **`@Marcus`** (Voice: *Warm Casual Male Tenor*)
  ```text
  34-year-old commuter with light-brown skin, short neat fade haircut, friendly expression, wearing a navy trench coat over a gray turtleneck, neutral 5600K studio lighting, clean slate-gray background, photorealistic portrait.
  ```

#### Ingredients (`Image > Nano Banana Pro`)

- **`@Plaza`**:
  ```text
  Empty European-style cobblestone twilight plaza at blue hour, warm string lights crisscrossed overhead between historic stone cafes, wet reflective cobblestones, 35mm lens, no people.
  ```
- **`@NovaStand`**:
  ```text
  Product close-up of a sleek matte-black acrylic tabletop payment stand with a glowing cyan NFC ring and minimalist white wordmark 'NOVAPAY' inside an open velvet-lined violin case, soft studio lighting.
  ```

### Step 3: Storyboard Frames (`Image > Nano Banana Pro` — Refer Only to `@Elena` & `@Marcus`)

- **Frame A.1 (Shot 1)**: `@Elena playing her wooden violin passionately in @Plaza under glowing string lights at blue hour, medium shot, 50mm lens.`
- **Frame A.2 (Shot 2)**: `@Marcus standing in @Plaza checking an empty leather wallet with a disappointed expression, medium close-up, 50mm lens.`
- **Frame A.3 (Shot 3 Start Frame)**: `@Marcus holding his smartphone above @NovaStand inside the open violin case in @Plaza, close-up, 50mm lens.`
- **Frame A.4 (Shot 3 End Frame)**: `Close-up of @NovaStand glowing bright emerald-green as the smartphone screen displays 'Tip Sent', warm string light bokeh.`

### Step 4: Animate Video & Dialogue (`Video > Omni Flash`, `6s`)

- **Clip A.1 (Shot 1)**: `Smooth arc shot around @Elena finishing a vibrant violin flourish in @Plaza as twilight string lights twinkle overhead, rich acoustic violin melody ending on a resonant chord.`
- **Clip A.2 (Shot 2)**: `Medium shot of @Marcus in @Plaza opening his leather wallet, seeing it empty, and spotting @NovaStand beside @Elena.`
- **Clip A.3 (Shot 3 — Frames Mode: Attach Frame A.3 + Frame A.4)**: `Close-up as @Marcus taps his smartphone against @NovaStand, triggering a soft emerald light pulse and a crisp musical confirmation chime.`
- **Clip A.4 (Shot 4 — Dialogue)**: `Over-the-shoulder shot from behind @Elena on the left looking at @Marcus on the right in @Plaza. @Marcus smiles and says: "No cash on me—glad you have NovaPay."`
- **Clip A.5 (Shot 5 — Dialogue)**: `Reverse-angle medium close-up of @Elena on the left looking screen-right in @Plaza, lowering her violin bow with a grateful smile. @Elena says: "Every note counts. Thank you!"`

### Step 5: Conversational Video Edit (`Omni Flash`) & Scenebuilder

- **Turn 1 Edit on Clip A.5**: `Warm up the overhead string lights to a golden 2500K glow and fade in clean white sans-serif text reading 'NOVAPAY — APPLAUSE, INSTANTLY.'`
- Add **Clips A.1 $\rightarrow$ A.5** to **Scenebuilder**, trim transitions, upscale to `720p`, and export.

## Bonus Pack B: "Kuntur — Above the Clouds" (Andean All-Weather Jacket)

### Step 1: 2-Sentence Story Seed

```text
Caught in a sudden freezing squall on a high Andean ridge before sunrise, wildlife photographer Mateo zips up his burnt-orange Kuntur shell and presses onward. As he crests the summit, the storm clouds part to reveal a breathtaking golden sea of clouds.
```

### Step 2: Create Character & Ingredients Once in Google Flow

#### Character (`Left Sidebar > Characters > New Character`)

- **`@Mateo`** (Voice: *Calm, Determined Male Baritone*)
  ```text
  35-year-old Peruvian wildlife photographer with sun-weathered copper-tan skin, high cheekbones, short windswept black hair, light stubble, wearing a charcoal fleece beanie, neutral 5600K studio lighting, clean slate-gray background, photorealistic portrait.
  ```

#### Ingredients (`Image > Nano Banana Pro`)

- **`@Ridge`**:
  ```text
  Empty high-altitude Andean mountain ridge at 5:50 AM, dark wet slate rock trail, swirling mist and dramatic cloud ceiling below, cold 7500K blue-gray storm light, 24mm wide-angle lens, no people.
  ```
- **`@KunturJacket`**:
  ```text
  Product studio shot of a technical alpine hardshell jacket in vibrant burnt-orange ripstop fabric with matte-black waterproof zippers and a minimalist silver condor emblem 'KUNTUR' on the left chest, water droplets beading on the fabric.
  ```

### Step 3: Storyboard Frames (`Image > Nano Banana Pro` — Refer Only to `@Mateo`)

- **Frame B.1 (Shot 1)**: `@Mateo wearing @KunturJacket on @Ridge in driving wind and sleet, zipping the collar up to his chin, medium close-up, 35mm lens.`
- **Frame B.2 (Shot 2 Start Frame)**: `Extreme macro close-up of sleet and rain droplets bouncing off the burnt-orange fabric of @KunturJacket worn by @Mateo on @Ridge.`
- **Frame B.3 (Shot 3 Start Frame)**: `Low-angle shot of @Mateo wearing @KunturJacket climbing the final rocky steps of @Ridge against dark swirling clouds.`
- **Frame B.4 (Shot 3 End Frame)**: `Wide shot of @Mateo wearing @KunturJacket standing at the summit of @Ridge as golden 2400K sunrise rays break over a vast sea of clouds.`

### Step 4: Animate Video & Dialogue (`Video > Omni Flash`, `6s`)

- **Clip B.1 (Shot 1)**: `Medium close-up of @Mateo wearing @KunturJacket on @Ridge, pulling the waterproof zipper up against howling mountain wind and sleet.`
- **Clip B.2 (Shot 2)**: `Macro slow-motion shot of freezing rain droplets beading and rolling cleanly off the chest emblem of @KunturJacket while @Mateo hikes forward.`
- **Clip B.3 (Shot 3 — Frames Mode: Attach Frame B.3 + Frame B.4)**: `Dynamic tracking shot following @Mateo in @KunturJacket as he crests @Ridge and the cold blue mist parts into blinding golden sunrise over the clouds.`
- **Clip B.4 (Shot 4 — Spoken Monologue)**: `Medium close-up of @Mateo in @KunturJacket at the sunlit summit of @Ridge, lowering his camera from his eye with a quiet smile and saying: "The storm never stops the climb. Kuntur."`

### Step 5: Conversational Video Edit (`Omni Flash`) & Scenebuilder

- **Turn 1 Edit on Clip B.4**: `Add a subtle warm anamorphic sunrise flare across the horizon and fade in bold minimalist white text reading 'KUNTUR — ABOVE EVERY STORM'.`
- Add **Clips B.1 $\rightarrow$ B.4** to **Scenebuilder**, upscale to `720p`, and export.
