# Bonus Google Flow Story Packs (Omni Flash + `@Character` Workflow)

> **Purpose**: Ready-to-run alternate commercial story packs for **Google Flow** ([labs.google/fx/tools/flow](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow)) using **100% real Google Flow features**:
> 1. **Step 1**: Short Story Seed & 5-Shot Beat Sheet (`Google Flow Agent`)
> 2. **Step 2**: Create Characters Once (`Left Sidebar > Characters > New Character` with bundled Voice)
> 3. **Step 3**: Generate Storyboard Images (`Image > Nano Banana Pro`) using `@Character` tags and **`More > Add to Prompt`** to keep backgrounds/props consistent
> 4. **Step 4**: Animate Video & Dialogue (`Video > Omni Flash`) using **`Video > Ingredients`** and **`Video > Frames` (`+ Add start frame` / `+ Add end frame`)**
> 5. **Step 5**: Conversational Video Edit (`Omni Flash` 3-turn editor) & **Scenebuilder**

---

## Bonus Pack A: "NovaPay — The Street-Market Violinist" (Tap-to-Tip App)

### Step 1: 2-Sentence Story Seed (`Google Flow Agent`)

```text
At dusk in a bustling cobblestone plaza, street violinist Elena finishes a passionate performance only to see a commuter realize he has no cash in his wallet. With a quick smile, he taps his phone against her NovaPay stand, triggering an instant chime that lights up her evening.
```

### Step 2: Create Characters Once in Google Flow (`Characters > New Character`)

- **`@Elena`** (Voice: *Expressive Young Female Soprano*)
  ```text
  26-year-old street violinist with warm bronze skin, dark braided hair wrapped in a crown, expressive dark-brown eyes, wearing an emerald-green wool coat and a cream knit scarf, neutral 5600K studio lighting, clean slate-gray background, photorealistic portrait.
  ```
- **`@Marcus`** (Voice: *Warm Casual Male Tenor*)
  ```text
  34-year-old commuter with light-brown skin, short neat fade haircut, friendly expression, wearing a navy trench coat over a gray turtleneck, neutral 5600K studio lighting, clean slate-gray background, photorealistic portrait.
  ```

### Step 3: Storyboard Images (`Image > Nano Banana Pro` — Refer Only to `@Elena` & `@Marcus`)

- **Image A.1 (Shot 1)**: `@Elena playing her wooden violin passionately in a European-style cobblestone plaza under glowing overhead string lights at blue hour, medium shot, 50mm lens.`
- **Image A.2 (Shot 2 — Click `More > Add to Prompt` on Image A.1 for plaza consistency)**: `@Marcus standing in the cobblestone plaza under the string lights checking an empty leather wallet with a disappointed expression, medium close-up, 50mm lens.`
- **Image A.3 (Shot 3 Start Frame — Click `More > Add to Prompt` on Image A.1)**: `@Marcus holding his smartphone above a sleek matte-black acrylic 'NOVAPAY' NFC payment stand resting inside an open velvet-lined violin case on the cobblestones, close-up, 50mm lens.`
- **Image A.4 (Shot 3 End Frame — Click `More > Add to Prompt` on Image A.3)**: `Close-up of the matte-black 'NOVAPAY' stand glowing bright emerald-green as the smartphone screen displays 'Tip Sent', warm string light bokeh.`

### Step 4: Animate Video & Dialogue (`Video > Omni Flash`, `6s`)

- **Clip A.1 (Shot 1 — Attach Image A.1 as `+ Add start frame`)**: `Smooth arc shot around @Elena finishing a vibrant violin flourish in the twilight cobblestone plaza as string lights twinkle overhead, rich acoustic violin melody ending on a resonant chord.`
- **Clip A.2 (Shot 2 — Attach Image A.2 as `+ Add start frame`)**: `Medium shot of @Marcus in the plaza opening his leather wallet, seeing it empty, and looking down with a smile toward the open violin case.`
- **Clip A.3 (Shot 3 — `Video > Frames`: Attach Image A.3 as `+ Add start frame` + Image A.4 as `+ Add end frame`)**: `Close-up as @Marcus taps his smartphone against the matte-black NovaPay stand inside the violin case, triggering a soft emerald light pulse and a crisp musical confirmation chime.`
- **Clip A.4 (Shot 4 — `Video > Ingredients` with `@Marcus`, `@Elena` + Image A.1)**: `Over-the-shoulder shot from behind @Elena on the left looking at @Marcus on the right in the twilight plaza. @Marcus smiles and says: "No cash on me—glad you have NovaPay."`
- **Clip A.5 (Shot 5 — `Video > Ingredients` with `@Elena` + Image A.1)**: `Reverse-angle medium close-up of @Elena on the left looking screen-right in the plaza, lowering her violin bow with a grateful smile. @Elena says: "Every note counts. Thank you!"`

### Step 5: Conversational Video Edit (`Omni Flash`) & Scenebuilder

- **Turn 1 Edit on Clip A.5**: `Warm up the overhead string lights to a golden 2500K glow and fade in clean white sans-serif text reading 'NOVAPAY — APPLAUSE, INSTANTLY.'`
- Add **Clips A.1 $\rightarrow$ A.5** to **Scenebuilder** (`More > Add to Scene`), trim transitions, upscale to `720p` (0 credits), and export.

## Bonus Pack B: "Kuntur — Above the Clouds" (Andean All-Weather Jacket)

### Step 1: 2-Sentence Story Seed (`Google Flow Agent`)

```text
Caught in a sudden freezing squall on a high Andean ridge before sunrise, wildlife photographer Mateo zips up his burnt-orange Kuntur shell and presses onward. As he crests the summit, the storm clouds part to reveal a breathtaking golden sea of clouds.
```

### Step 2: Create Character Once in Google Flow (`Characters > New Character`)

- **`@Mateo`** (Voice: *Calm, Determined Male Baritone*)
  ```text
  35-year-old Peruvian wildlife photographer with sun-weathered copper-tan skin, high cheekbones, short windswept black hair, light stubble, wearing a charcoal fleece beanie and a technical alpine hardshell jacket in vibrant burnt-orange ripstop fabric with matte-black waterproof zippers and a silver condor emblem 'KUNTUR' on the left chest, neutral 5600K studio lighting, clean slate-gray background, photorealistic portrait.
  ```

### Step 3: Storyboard Images (`Image > Nano Banana Pro` — Refer Only to `@Mateo`)

- **Image B.1 (Shot 1)**: `@Mateo hiking on a high-altitude Andean mountain ridge at 5:50 AM in driving wind and sleet, zipping his burnt-orange collar up to his chin, cold 7500K blue-gray storm light, medium close-up, 35mm lens.`
- **Image B.2 (Shot 2 — Click `More > Add to Prompt` on Image B.1)**: `Extreme macro close-up of sleet and rain droplets beading and bouncing off the burnt-orange ripstop chest fabric and silver 'KUNTUR' condor emblem worn by @Mateo on the stormy ridge.`
- **Image B.3 (Shot 3 Start Frame — Click `More > Add to Prompt` on Image B.1)**: `Low-angle shot of @Mateo climbing the final wet slate steps of the Andean ridge against dark swirling storm clouds, 24mm wide-angle lens.`
- **Image B.4 (Shot 3 End Frame — Click `More > Add to Prompt` on Image B.3)**: `Wide shot of @Mateo standing at the rocky summit of the Andean ridge as golden 2400K sunrise rays break over a vast sea of clouds below.`

### Step 4: Animate Video & Dialogue (`Video > Omni Flash`, `6s`)

- **Clip B.1 (Shot 1 — Attach Image B.1 as `+ Add start frame`)**: `Medium close-up of @Mateo on the stormy Andean ridge, pulling his waterproof jacket zipper up against howling mountain wind and sleet.`
- **Clip B.2 (Shot 2 — Attach Image B.2 as `+ Add start frame`)**: `Macro slow-motion shot of freezing rain droplets beading and rolling cleanly off the burnt-orange fabric and 'KUNTUR' chest emblem while @Mateo hikes forward.`
- **Clip B.3 (Shot 3 — `Video > Frames`: Attach Image B.3 as `+ Add start frame` + Image B.4 as `+ Add end frame`)**: `Dynamic tracking shot following @Mateo as he crests the rocky summit ridge and the cold blue mist parts into blinding golden sunrise over the sea of clouds.`
- **Clip B.4 (Shot 4 — `Video > Ingredients` with `@Mateo` + Image B.4)**: `Medium close-up of @Mateo at the sunlit summit ridge, lowering his camera from his eye with a quiet smile and saying: "The storm never stops the climb. Kuntur."`

### Step 5: Conversational Video Edit (`Omni Flash`) & Scenebuilder

- **Turn 1 Edit on Clip B.4**: `Add a subtle warm anamorphic sunrise flare across the horizon and fade in bold minimalist white text reading 'KUNTUR — ABOVE EVERY STORM'.`
- Add **Clips B.1 $\rightarrow$ B.4** to **Scenebuilder**, upscale to `720p`, and export.
